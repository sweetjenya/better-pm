Better PM for MNF Club — Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

Better PM is a browser extension that shows MNF Club private messages in a larger panel with a local, searchable archive.

What it handles

The private messages, contact list and profile details that the MNF Club game page receives while you are logged in.

Where it keeps them

Only in your browser's extension storage on your computer. Nothing is sent to the developer or to any third party. The extension has no server of its own, no analytics and no tracking.

What it sends

Only the requests the MNF Club game itself makes (opening a conversation, sending a message you wrote, refreshing statuses), to MNF Club's own servers, while you use the panel.

Your control

"Export archive" in the panel menu saves your archive to a file you keep. Removing the extension deletes the stored archive.

Affiliation

The extension is unofficial and is not affiliated with MNF Club.
